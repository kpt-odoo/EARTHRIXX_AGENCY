(ABOUT)

THE PERSON BEHIND THE CUTS

I'm EARTHRIXX — a video editor and colorist who believes the edit is the story. Six years in, I've cut everything from 15-second spots to 40-minute documentaries, and I still get goosebumps when a cut lands perfectly.

My work lives at the intersection of rhythm and emotion: music videos that hit like the track, commercials that sell without shouting, documentaries that breathe. I work closely with directors, agencies and creators — from the first assembly to the final grade — and I sweat every frame in between.

When I'm not editing, I'm reverse-engineering trailers frame by frame, hunting for the perfect LUT, or shooting b-roll nobody asked for. If you have footage and a feeling, I can turn it into a film.
